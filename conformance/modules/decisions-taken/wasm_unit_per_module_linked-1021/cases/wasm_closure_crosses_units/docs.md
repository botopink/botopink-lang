# wasm_closure_crosses_units

A function value crosses between two modules of a program: a closure made in `maker` called in `main`, one made in `main` called in `maker`, one passed to `std@array`'s `map`, a type's method taken as a value, one capturing `maker`'s module `val`, and closures of eleven parameters made and called on either side — the same answers on every target. On wasm each module is a unit of its own and a closure a GC value holding its entry's `funcref`, called by `call_ref` through `$sig_<n>`, a rec group of its own after the prelude's in every unit, with no cap on the arity (decisions 1055 (4), 1076 (2)).
